# School evidence plan

## Evaluation question

Does the app help learners identify learning conditions, choose a strategy, and reflect on what helps them learn?

## Collect only

- anonymous participant code, not full name;
- before and after scores for understanding, self-observation, strategy experimentation, and reflection;
- before and after confidence ratings from 1 to 5;
- number of observations, experiments, and reflections;
- optional short feedback: what was useful and what should improve.

## Do not collect without school permission

- names, contact details, passwords, or location;
- sensitive personal journal entries;
- health or diagnostic information.

## Evidence to include in the project report

1. Number of pilot participants.
2. Average before and after scores for the four learning behaviours, plus confidence change.
3. Counts of completed observations, experiments, and reflections.
4. Anonymous learner feedback themes.
5. Screenshots of the phone interface and testing results.

## Collection flow

1. Obtain consent/permission and use the anonymous participant code.
2. Complete Understand Myself: interests, strengths, challenges, attention, feelings, learning conditions, and a personal question.
3. Complete the starting self-check and confidence rating.
4. Learn reliable principles, then complete an observation and one experiment: usual approach, different strategy, comparison, and next step.
5. Complete a reflection describing what happened and what to adjust.
6. Complete the same after self-check and confidence rating.
7. Download the personal evidence report to compare before and after results.

Each behaviour is self-scored: 0 = not yet demonstrated, 1 = partly demonstrated, and 2 = clearly demonstrated. The total is out of 8. The app reports change in the specific behaviours measured; it does not diagnose a condition, measure neuroplasticity directly, or prove broad educational effects.
